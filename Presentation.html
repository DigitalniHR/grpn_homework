Evaluation design

There is no ground truth, so we test whether the rating withstands a challenge

01Target ordering

Do higher ratings show higher KPI results and fewer people below target?

monotonicity
02Dominance contradictions

Within one peer group, is a much higher-rated person worse on every available signal?

pairwise consistency
03Obvious contradictions

How many ratings 4–5 have a KPI result below 75% without counter-evidence?

edge cases
04Stability

When activity is split into odd and even weeks, do people remain within one rating point?

sensitivity
This supports a claim of better internal defensibility, not objective predictive accuracy.

Brief · fairness / gaming / handoff

Limits and handoff

The model stays useful only if its risks remain visible

RiskCurrent mitigation
Metric gamingEffort instead of task count; reviewed PR files only; activity capped at 25%.
Score becomes the goalManager context and a human final decision remain mandatory.
Period mismatchVisible limitation in the prototype; production must align rating and evidence windows.
Weak peer groupsFlag groups below three complete profiles and review the benchmark.
Wrong identityUse confirmed employee IDs only; unresolved evidence never becomes low performance.
Unequal observabilityRole-aware evidence policy; no requirement that every role generates Engineering-like traces.
